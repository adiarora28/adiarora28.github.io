About

I like numbers, and supply chain is a field where numbers decide things: what to make, what to stock and when to move it.

I spent more than five years as an SAP IBP consultant on demand planning, inventory optimisation, supply planning and S&OP. I configured and tuned forecasting models for clients, and I wanted to understand how the algorithms behind them work. So I'm doing an MSc in Data Science at EPITA, where I now build the models, pipelines and evaluations myself.

My goal is to build forecasting and machine-learning systems that help planners and teams make better decisions, in an industry where the numbers matter.

A stage stayed in only if it raised recall. The final pipeline answers in about 3 seconds per query, against 0.5 seconds for plain vector search.

  • The agent detects low-confidence retrieval, rephrases the query and retries. It uses a safe calculator for arithmetic instead of guessing.
  • Tested prompt injection with a poisoned chunk, wrapped retrieved text as untrusted context, and put a human approval step in front of the Slack action tool (MCP).
  • Kept a failure log that separates retrieval misses from generation errors. Answer accuracy on the internal eval was 88%.

Flight delay prediction

Statistical Analysis and ML · solo · 2026

Predicts whether a US domestic flight lands more than 15 minutes late. KNN imputation, encoding and a class-weighted logistic regression sit in a scikit-learn Pipeline that prevents train/test leakage, since only about 20% of flights are delayed. Evaluated with precision, recall, F1, AUC-ROC and a confusion matrix, and served in a Streamlit app.

  • scikit-learn
